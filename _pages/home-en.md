## Fire Safety & Built Environment Research

**SAFE-T Lab** is a research group focusing on fire safety, structural fire engineering, evacuation safety, and fire protection systems and policy.  
This website serves as the personal academic page of **Prof. Jaeyeong Lee** and the official webpage of the laboratory at Dong-Eui University.

### Research Areas

| Area | Topics |
|---|---|
| **Fire Safety Engineering** | Fire dynamics, heat release rate, fire risk, and fire investigation |
| **Structural Fire Engineering** | High-temperature properties of concrete, fire resistance, and residual performance |
| **Evacuation & Urban Safety** | Evacuation safety, urban rail systems, elderly evacuation, and spatial analysis |
| **Fire Protection Systems & Policy** | Fire protection systems, codes and standards, policy, and disaster management |

### Research Software

We develop and publish research-oriented calculation and analysis tools.

- **Concrete Thermal Conductivity Estimator** — estimation of temperature-dependent thermal conductivity and piecewise equations
- **HRR Curve Analysis Tool** — heat release rate curve analysis and modeling
- Additional tools will be released after development and validation.
