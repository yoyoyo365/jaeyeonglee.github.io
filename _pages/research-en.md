## 1. Fire Safety Engineering

Research on fire growth, combustion characteristics, heat release rate (HRR), fire risk assessment, and fire investigation.

## 2. Structural Fire Engineering

Research on high-temperature thermal properties of concrete, temperature distribution, fire resistance, and post-fire residual performance.

## 3. Evacuation & Urban Safety

Research on evacuation safety in buildings and urban rail systems, occupant density, elderly evacuation, and GIS-based spatial analysis.

## 4. Fire Protection Systems & Policy

Research on fire protection systems, fire safety codes and standards, institutional policy, and disaster management systems.
